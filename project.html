Loller

Multi-residential
Seoul, South Korea
2025
Completed

Situated in the heart of Itaewon, Loller is a collection of six residential apartments with an emphasis on honest materiality and a distinctive identity, built for a contemporary urban context blending tradition with innovation.

Country South Korea
Client Private
Year 2025
Architecture Rojdi Alici

Inspired by the tension between permanence and lightness to maximise spatial flexibility.

About the project

Responding to the local context, the architectural language blended provides a thoughtful response to the scale and character of the streetscape, with heritage-inspired materials combined with the clean edges.

Influenced by residential ideas, embracing views of local's collective memory, architecture is the translation of space, in an equilibrium with the surrounding space.

About the project

The interior experience frames light and views with careful layering. Each home has been carefully crafted to enhance daily ritual and provide a sense of quiet sophistication.

About the project

With special consideration to all aspects of design, the project emphasises material honesty, spatial generosity, and a restrained palette to ensure enduring quality.
