Garden of Knowledge

Library
Seoul, South Korea
2023

My bachelor project reimagines the modern library through the Renaissance concept of the "Memory Theater," conceived by Giulio Camillo. Like its historical precedent, this library is designed as a physical architecture that organizes and maps a universe of knowledge.

The design translates this idea into a contemporary spatial metaphor: a solar system of knowledge. The library's main floor is an open landscape where major disciplines like literature, science, religion are housed in distinct pavilions, or "planets." These are interconnected by a network of walkable, organic pathways, allowing visitors to navigate the collection as if exploring a planetary system. Within each section, smaller "bookhouses" with bark-like textures offer quiet nests for focused study, grounding the grand concept in the intimate act of reading.

Country South Korea
Client Bachelor Thesis
Year 2023
Architecture Rojdi Alici

The Memory Theater, originally conceived as a wooden structure housing all human knowledge, becomes the conceptual engine of this library. Each pavilion embodies a branch of understanding, a planet in its own orbit, while the connective pathways mirror the associative nature of thought itself.

"A solar system of knowledge, where every discipline orbits the act of reading."

Each pavilion functions as a self-contained world, a planet dedicated to a single field of knowledge. Their forms emerge from organic geometries, echoing the natural growth patterns found in forests and root systems. The smaller bookhouses nested within each pavilion feature bark-like textured walls, creating intimate alcoves that invite focused, solitary reading amidst the library's expansive landscape.
