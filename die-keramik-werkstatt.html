Cultural / Workshop
Goriçko, Slovenia
2022
Completed

This project is a studio for potters, designed to be in direct dialogue with the landscape of Goriçko, Slovenia. The core concept is to create an environment that supports the entire creative process, from contemplation of the natural surroundings to the focused, physical act of making.

The building’s form is driven by two key elements. First, a series of large, arched openings at each workstation act as deliberate frames, capturing views and drawing natural light deep into the studio. Second, the floor plan is organized into two distinct wings for ‘planning’ and ‘creation,’ separating clean, quiet tasks from the hands-on work. A central open-air atrium connects these wings, serving as both the main circulation path and the communal heart of the studio.
