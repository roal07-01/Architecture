Haus der Jugend

Student Housing
Graz, Austria
2024
Completed

To meet the need for both affordable student housing and shared campus facilities, this project organizes its functions by floor. The ground level is designed as an open, public base containing a café, workshops, and a gym for all students to use. Above this base, the building holds the private residential floors.

This clear separation of spaces, based on the real needs of students, creates a balance between group activity and private life. The result is a complete environment that supports all aspects of being a student. The building's design is straightforward and practical, with a visible structure and internal systems chosen for durability and easy upkeep.

Country Austria
Client Academic
Year 2024
Architecture Rojdi Alici

“A building that organizes community by elevation — public life at ground, private life above.”

The vertical organization concept drives the entire design. Public program occupies the ground floor, creating an active, permeable base. Above, the residential floors step back to provide privacy and daylight. Each level responds to a specific need, giving the building a legible logic from the outside in.
